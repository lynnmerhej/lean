Why these programs exist

I train people in person. Along the way I kept meeting people who know their way around a gym but don't know how to build a plan, or who don't have the time to put one together.

These programs are that plan, already built. You open the plan, follow the sessions, and skip the guesswork.

They're temporary starter plans, general on purpose. A program built around your own needs is always better, and that's what I do with my clients.

They're not for everyone

Each program is the same for everyone who buys it. It works best if you know the main lifts and can follow a plan on your own.

If you're brand new to training, have an injury, or need something built around your schedule, don't buy one of these. Message me and we'll find what fits.
