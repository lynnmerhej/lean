Inside the week

Every session opens with the same 8-10 minute warm-up. Tap a day to see the focus and the first lifts.

How the four weeks progress

Week 1Learn the liftsLower rep range, stop with about 3 reps left in the tank.
Week 2Add loadAdd a little weight or 1-2 reps, with about 2 reps left.
Week 3Add volumeAn extra set on the first two exercises each day, with 1-2 reps left.
Week 4Push the top setsLast set of each main lift goes close to failure, with good form.

Questions

What equipment do I need?

A barbell, dumbbells, a bench, a cable machine, and a few machines such as leg press and leg extension. The PDF lists home swaps that need only a bench or couch, dumbbells, and resistance bands.

How do I get the program?

You'll get a download link right after payment. The program is a PDF you can keep on your phone.

Can I get a program made just for me?

Yes. Message me on WhatsApp or Instagram with your goal, how many days you can train, and your equipment. Personalized programs are separate from this $30 one and priced individually. I also train people in person.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $30 once.

What if I have an injury or a medical condition?

Check with your doctor or physiotherapist before starting any training program.

What do I do after four weeks?

Repeat the program with heavier weights, or move on to the next program when it's released.
